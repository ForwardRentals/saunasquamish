FAQ

Squamish sauna questions, answered

Is there a public sauna in Squamish?

There's no standing public bathhouse in Squamish, BC. Instead, Mamquam Sauna operates a mobile wood-fired barrel sauna that's delivered to a private location for your booking window, in Squamish, Whistler, or Pemberton.

What does a mobile sauna rental in Squamish include?

A full 24-hour private rental includes the wood-fired barrel sauna, a cold plunge tub, all the firewood you need, towels, and a startup guide. It runs $325 per session.

How hot does the sauna get?

The original barrel sauna from the Mamquam River reaches up to 90°C, heated the traditional way with wood, not electric elements.

Can I get a sauna delivered to Whistler or Pemberton too?

Yes — the same mobile sauna rental serves Squamish, Whistler, and Pemberton across the Sea to Sky corridor.

Can I have it delivered anywhere I want?

No — there's no fixed spot Mamquam Sauna sets up at, and delivery can't happen on public property. The location has to be private property, and it's approved directly with the team before your session is confirmed. Reach out with where you'd like it set up and they'll let you know if it works.
