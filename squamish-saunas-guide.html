Squamish Saunas: Everything You Need to Know

A practical guide to sauna culture in Squamish, BC — where to find one, what it costs, and how a mobile sauna rental actually works.

Search "saunas Squamish" or "sauna Squamish" and you'll notice something: there's no spa row, no bathhouse district, no strip of storefront saunas. Squamish's sauna scene is smaller and, honestly, better — built around one hand-crafted, wood-fired barrel sauna that travels to wherever you're staying.

Is there a public sauna in Squamish?

Not in the traditional sense. Squamish doesn't have a standalone public bathhouse or sauna facility you can walk into. What it does have is Mamquam Sauna, a mobile sauna rental delivered to a private location for your booking window. There's no fixed spot they set up at, and delivery can't happen on public land — the location has to be private property, approved in advance directly with the Mamquam Sauna team.

How is the delivery location arranged?

You don't just pick a spot and book — delivery has to be requested and approved. Reach out with where you'd like the sauna set up (your own property, a rental you're staying at with the owner's OK, etc.) and Glenn will confirm whether it works before your session is locked in.

Where did the Squamish sauna scene start?

The story starts on the banks of the Mamquam River, where a hand-built barrel sauna existed for years as a local secret — no signs, no bookings, just steam and the sound of the river. When it was sold off, local operator Glenn bought the original sauna, restored it, and turned it into a proper mobile rental so the tradition didn't disappear.

How much does a sauna rental in Squamish cost?

A full 24-hour session runs $325 flat. That includes the wood-fired barrel sauna, a cold plunge tub, all the firewood you'll need, towels, and a startup guide — there's no separate delivery fee or cold plunge upcharge.

What's the difference between a mobile sauna rental and a spa sauna?

A spa sauna is usually electric, shared with other guests, and on a fixed schedule. A mobile sauna rental like the one behind this guide is wood-fired (reaching up to 90°C), delivered directly to you, and booked privately for a full 24 hours — no strangers, no shared timeslots.

Can I book a sauna in Whistler or Pemberton too?

Yes. The same mobile sauna serves the whole Sea to Sky corridor — Squamish, Whistler, and Pemberton — so it works whether you're based in town or up the highway. See the Whistler sauna guide for more on sessions up the valley.

What should I bring to a sauna session?

Swimwear, a robe or warm layer for between rounds, water, and a felt sauna hat. A wool or felt hat keeps your head cooler so you can stay in the heat longer — Sauna Hats Canada ships them from Squamish. Firewood, towels and the cold plunge are already included with the rental.

How do I book?

Head to mamquamsauna.com and reserve a date directly, or send an inquiry through the mobile sauna rental page on this site if you have questions first.
